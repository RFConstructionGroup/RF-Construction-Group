RF CONSTRUCTION GROUP WEBSITE — UPDATED WITH YOUR PROJECT PHOTOS

Open index.html to preview locally.

UPLOAD TO GITHUB PAGES:
1. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js, README.txt and the entire images folder to the ROOT of your repository.
2. Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from a branch -> main -> /(root) -> Save.

The site includes your RF Construction Group logo, project gallery, category filters, lightbox photo viewer, service sections, estimate form, and mobile call button.
The estimate form uses the visitor's email app and sends to buildwithrf@gmail.com.
